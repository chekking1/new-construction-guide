Why I start first time buyers with new construction

  • It's the most affordable path right now

    In the current market, new construction is the most affordable way into a home that I see for first time buyers.

  • Builder incentives make the payment work

    Builders pay to lower your interest rate (a rate buydown) or cover part of your closing costs (a closing cost credit). That's what turns a home you love into a payment that works for your first home.

  • Your neighbors are at your stage of life

    New communities skew young and family oriented. You're choosing the people on your street as much as the floor plan.

For my buyers, that has meant saving hundreds a month on the payment, plus thousands in closing costs they would otherwise have to bring to the closing table.

How a 7 year ARM works

ARM stands for adjustable rate mortgage. Your rate is locked for the first 7 years. After that, it can go up or down with the market.

How the EdgeHomes 3% works

A seller concession is money the builder puts toward your costs. You can use Edge's 3% for closing costs, or for a temporary rate buydown on top of the ARM, which lowers your rate even further in the early years. Stacking the two adds serious monthly savings, and that's just one of the ways to use it.

The builders, rates, and incentives shown are examples as of September 23, 2026 and can change at any moment. Each builder sets its own terms, qualifications, and eligible homes. This is general information, not a loan offer.

Why most of these are on the outskirts

Real estate has always followed what I call the law of compromise. There are 3 things every buyer wants:

Price. Floorplan. Location. Pick 2.

  • Best price and floorplan: you might compromise on location.
  • Best floorplan and location: you might compromise on price.
  • Best price and location: you might compromise on floorplan.

New construction incentives soften that blow, but the rule still holds. Even with an incentive, the same house in Lehi is going to cost you far more than it would in Eagle Mountain.
